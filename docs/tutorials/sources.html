KfW 458 Applicant Eligibility Pilot

Decision question

For a natural-person Case, determine whether the supplied applicant facts satisfy the modeled applicant-eligibility rules of KfW product 458 under the Rule Set Version selected by the explicit Decision Date.

eligible means only a positive answer to this scoped applicant question. It is not a statement about measure eligibility, application completeness, funding amount, KfW approval, available budget, or legal entitlement.

Source selection review

The official KfW document catalogue identifies a July 2026 revision and retains the December 2025 revision separately. Both PDFs state their validity dates. Page 2 of the December revision lists the GbR exclusion but no usufruct exclusion; page 2 of the July revision adds that exclusion. Therefore identical modeled usufruct-applicant Facts yield eligible on 2026-07-20 and ineligible on 2026-07-21. The July PDF declares Stand 07/2026, Bestellnummer 600 000 5131, and validity from 2026-07-21. Its page 2 contains the selected applicant categories and explicit exclusions. Page 8 separately requires self-using owners claiming the climate-speed or income bonus to submit a land-register excerpt as ownership Evidence. The recorded lengths and SHA-256 values identify the exact current bytes reverified on 2026-09-08 and the archived bytes reviewed on 2026-09-08.

The Merkblatt states that the program is based on the BEG EM guideline, but the pilot deliberately compiles only the product-specific applicant passage. If a later Rule depends on BEG EM, technical requirements, FAQs, or another KfW document, that document must be registered as its own pinned Source Revision and cited explicitly; authority is never inherited through an unmodeled link.

The version catalogue is evidence for document identity and version history; it does not itself supply compiled eligibility Rules. Both selected Merkblatt revisions are external Source Revisions. An authored Spec must represent and cite each selected statement before it can produce a generated requirement or result.

Pilot boundary

The first slice supports only the natural-person category and the applicant facts needed to distinguish ownership from the explicitly excluded GbR and usufruct cases for an existing residential building in Germany. A negative naturalPerson Fact means only that this scoped category is not satisfied; other applicant categories, including a Wohnungseigentümergemeinschaft, require their own Decision Contract and are not assessed as program-wide ineligible.

The pilot produces:

  • eligible when a unique positive applicant-eligibility conclusion is entailed;
  • ineligible when a source-backed applicant exclusion or failed necessary applicant condition entails that conclusion;
  • unknown when one or more named Fact Gaps prevent a unique conclusion;
  • one source-justified Next Question, selected deterministically from the current Fact Gaps while the outcome remains unknown;
  • an independent ownership Evidence Requirement Evaluation of fulfilled, missing, or not_applicable, with classifications for supplied Evidence and its own page-8 Source Citation;
  • an error for input outside the pilot boundary, conflicting Facts, ambiguous Rules, or a missing or non-unique applicable Rule Set Version.

Version Selection Conventions v1 use only the stable Rule Set, the explicit Decision Date, and the authored half-open Validity Periods. The caller cannot pin a version and no today/latest default exists.

Explicitly excluded from the pilot

  • technical eligibility of a heating system or measure;
  • subsidy rates, bonuses, eligible costs, calculations, and combinations;
  • application timing, contracts, forms, document upload/storage, automated document-content or authenticity validation, or approval;
  • automatic ingestion, interpretation, or monitoring of KfW documents;
  • coverage of other KfW, BAFA, state, federal, or EU programs;
  • legal, financial, or funding advice and any promise of approval;
  • a conversational user interface or production deployment.

Cloud Privileged-Access Compliance Slice

Decision question and sources

For one supplied Cloud System profile, determine whether the two authored controls IA-2 and IA-2(1) from NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 are satisfied, violated, or unknown under the Rule Set Version selected by the explicit Decision Date.

The authored citations point to IA-2 and IA-2(1) on PDF page 158 (printed page 132) and to the IA-02(01) assessment objective and methods on PDF pages 255–256 (printed pages 244–245). The compiler does not fetch or interpret these documents. It verifies the explicit revision identities and compiles the authored statements.

The complete positive Question Flow Case starts without Rule Facts, asks only the currently decision-relevant questions, and reaches satisfied after the authored answers are submitted. Separate Cases prove violated and unknown, all three Evidence Requirement statuses, and all three Obligation statuses. The MFA Evidence Requirement remains independent: missing assessment evidence does not turn a satisfied policy Condition into a violation.
